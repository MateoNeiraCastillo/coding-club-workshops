# Build Your AI Clone

Analyze a WhatsApp chat with Python and build an AI that texts like you. Then see if your friends can tell who's real.

**Coding Club Workshop · Friday, 27 November 2026**

## Open the notebook

[![Open In Colab](https://colab.research.google.com/assets/colab-badge.svg)](https://colab.research.google.com/github/MateoNeiraCastillo/coding-club-workshops/blob/main/01-ai-clone/notebooks/solutions.ipynb)

Then go to **File → Save a copy in Drive** and run the cells in order with **Shift + Enter**.

## What you build

1. **Read your chat** — upload an exported WhatsApp chat, or use the sample one
2. **Your Wrapped** — who texts the most and your top words
3. **Your Style DNA** — your signature phrase, reply speed, who starts the conversations and your vibe according to the AI
4. **Your AI clone** — Gemini learns your style from your real messages
5. **Chat with your clone** — with a link you can send to your friends
6. **Real or Clone?** — a game to see if people can tell your real replies from your clone's

Along the way you learn the basics of Python: variables, functions, lists, dictionaries, loops and conditions, with 4 small challenges. The harder code (reading WhatsApp exports, charts, the chat window) lives in [`workshop.py`](workshop.py), so the notebook stays short and easy to follow.

## What you need

- A laptop and a personal Google account (18+ to use the AI part)
- An exported WhatsApp chat (**without media**), or the sample chat
  - **iPhone:** open the chat → tap the name → **Export Chat**
  - **Android:** open the chat → ⋮ → **More** → **Export chat**

## The sample chat

Don't want to use your own chat? Use **"Mentors Anonymous"**, a group chat between **Tony Stark, Yoda and Haymitch**. All the messages are original and were written for this workshop. Pick one of them and clone him!

## Privacy

- To create your clone, a sample of your messages is sent to **Google's Gemini**. On the free plan, Google may keep them and people at Google may read them.
- You choose how many messages to send, and you can remove any message that contains words you don't want to share.
- Only **your own** messages are sent, never your friends'.
- Never upload a real chat to this repository.
